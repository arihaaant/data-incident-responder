## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.orders`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream

**Evidence**

The unique_stg_orders_order_key test failed with 300 duplicate rows. raw.orders contains duplicate o_orderkey values (e.g., order_key 800 appears twice with identical column values). Adding DISTINCT in stg_orders removes these duplicate rows, making order_key unique and allowing the test to pass.
