## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.fct_orders`
- **Cause:** a change in a model
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`

**Evidence**

The not_null_fct_orders_region_name test failed with 14,625 null region_name values. In fct_orders the join to dim_customers used o.order_key = c.customer_key, which mismatched rows and produced null region_name. Correcting the join to o.customer_key = c.customer_key aligns orders with their customers, eliminating nulls. After applying this change, try_patch reported no failing tests.
