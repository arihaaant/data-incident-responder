**Evidence**

The unique_fct_orders_order_key test failed with 12,900 duplicate rows. stg_orders has 15,000 distinct order_key values, but fct_orders also had 60,175 rows, indicating duplication. The items CTE originally grouped by order_key, line_number, producing multiple rows per order_key (one per line item). Joining this to stg_orders created duplicate order_key rows in fct_orders. Removing line_number from the GROUP BY makes items aggregated per order_key, eliminating duplicates and passing all checks.
