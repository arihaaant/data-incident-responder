## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.lineitem`, column `l_extendedprice`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_order_items`, `fct_orders`, `stg_lineitem`

**Evidence**

The not_null test on stg_lineitem_extended_price failed with 8,751 nulls. The nulls are present in the source data, so the problem originates upstream; downstream models cannot fix missing source values.
