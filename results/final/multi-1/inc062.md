# Incident inc062

**Status:** Escalated to the owner of raw.orders. Nothing was changed.

## What broke

- `model.tpch_warehouse.stg_orders` error: Compilation Error in model stg_orders (models/staging/stg_orders.sql)

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.orders`, column `o_orderdate`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`, `stg_orders`

**Evidence**

The stg_orders model selects o_orderdate (VARCHAR) directly as order_date, but its contract expects DATE. profile_column on raw.orders.o_orderdate shows 600 values cannot be converted to DATE (lossless: false). The model's SQL does not convert the column, causing a contract type mismatch error. Since conversion would be lossy, the issue must be fixed at the source; the staging model cannot safely patch it.

**Column profile** `raw.orders.o_orderdate` (varchar to date): would lose data: 600 values do not convert to date
