# Incident inc063

**Status:** Escalated to the owner of raw.customer. Nothing was changed.

## What broke

- `model.tpch_warehouse.stg_customers` error: Compilation Error in model stg_customers (models/staging/stg_customers.sql)

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.customer`, column `c_acctbal`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `dim_customers`, `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`, `stg_customers`

**Evidence**

The stg_customers model contract expects account_balance as DECIMAL(15,2) but the source raw.customer.c_acctbal is VARCHAR. profile_column shows conversion to DECIMAL(15,2) is not lossless (only 1 decimal place, contract requires 2). The model selects the column without casting, causing a contract type mismatch error. Since the source data cannot be losslessly converted, the problem originates upstream and must be escalated.

**Column profile** `raw.customer.c_acctbal` (varchar to decimal(15,2)): would lose data: values arrive with at most 1 decimal places where the contract keeps 2; the missing digits can't be recovered
