## What was done

Proposed a change to `models/staging/stg_orders.sql`. Removed the date filter that excluded orders after 1998-07-01. The original filter caused 210 recent orders to be dropped, leading to relationship check failures (801 lineitems referencing missing orders) and a freshness test failure. By selecting all rows from the raw.orders source, we preserve the full dataset and satisfy downstream checks while keeping column names and types unchanged.

```diff
--- a/models/staging/stg_orders.sql
+++ b/models/staging/stg_orders.sql
@@ -7,4 +7,3 @@
     o_orderpriority as priority,
     _loaded_at as loaded_at
 from {{ source('raw', 'orders') }}
-where o_orderdate < date '1998-07-01'
```

- **Tested in a copy of the warehouse:** every check passed.
- **Reviewer:** approved. Fix removes the erroneous date filter that caused data loss and downstream relationship failures. It restores the full orders dataset without masking or altering data semantics.
