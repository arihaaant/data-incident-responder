## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.stg_lineitem`
- **Cause:** a change in a model
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_order_items`, `fct_orders`

**Evidence**

The unique_stg_lineitem_order_line_id test failed with 14902 duplicate order_line_id values. The stg_lineitem model used a UNION ALL that duplicated rows where l_returnflag = 'R', causing non‑unique order_line_id. Removing the UNION ALL eliminates the duplicates and the test passes (verified with try_patch).
