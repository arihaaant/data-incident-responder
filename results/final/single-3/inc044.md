## What broke

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.fct_daily_revenue`
- **Cause:** a change in a model

**Evidence**

The failing test assert_daily_revenue_one_row_per_region expects exactly one row per (order_date, region_name). The current fct_daily_revenue groups by order_date, region_name, priority, producing multiple rows per region per day (one per priority), causing 3659 duplicate rows. Upstream models (fct_orders, dim_customers, etc.) have no duplicate issues. The fix is to remove priority from the SELECT and GROUP BY, ensuring a single row per day and region.
