**Status:** Fix proposed. Needs a human review before it is merged.

## What broke

- `unique_fct_orders_order_key` fail (12900 rows): Got 12900 results, configured to fail if != 0

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.fct_orders`
- **Cause:** a change in a model
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`

**Evidence**

The unique_fct_orders_order_key test failed with 12,900 duplicate order_key rows. In fct_orders, the items CTE grouped by order_key and line_number, producing multiple rows per order_key (one per line item). The subsequent left join on order_key duplicated the order rows, violating uniqueness. Removing line_number from the GROUP BY aggregates items per order_key, ensuring a single row per order_key and making the uniqueness test pass.
