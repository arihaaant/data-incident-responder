**Evidence**

The `stg_orders` model applies a filter `where year(o_orderdate) < 1998`, which drops orders from later years that still have lineitem rows, causing the mismatch and also triggering the `assert_orders_fresh` failure (1 row). Removing the year filter restores all order keys, fixing both failures. A trial patch without the filter (`try_patch`) succeeded with all checks passing.
