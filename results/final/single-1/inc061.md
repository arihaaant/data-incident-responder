# Incident inc061

**Status:** Escalated to the owner of raw.lineitem. Nothing was changed.

## What broke

- `model.tpch_warehouse.stg_lineitem` error: Compilation Error in model stg_lineitem (models/staging/stg_lineitem.sql)

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.lineitem`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_order_items`, `fct_orders`, `stg_lineitem`

**Evidence**

The stg_lineitem model failed contract checks: column extended_price is BIGINT but contract expects DECIMAL(15,2), and net_revenue is DECIMAL(35,2) but contract expects DECIMAL(18,4). Profiling raw.lineitem.l_extendedprice shows it is BIGINT with no fractional part, and the conversion to the contracted DECIMAL(15,2) is not lossless (fractions have been dropped). This indicates a source data type/format change that caused loss of precision, which cannot be recovered in downstream models. Therefore the problem originated at the source raw.lineitem table, and the appropriate response is to escalate to the source owner.

**Column profile** `raw.lineitem.l_extendedprice` (bigint to decimal(15,2)): would lose data: no value has a fractional part although the contract keeps 2 decimal places; if the source used to send fractions, they were dropped before arriving

## What was done

No change was made to the warehouse. The data has to be corrected where it comes from.
