# Incident inc022

**Status:** Escalated to the owner of model.dim_customers. Nothing was changed.

## What broke

- `not_null_fct_orders_region_name` fail (1892 rows): Got 1892 results, configured to fail if != 0

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.dim_customers`
- **Cause:** a change in a model
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`

**Evidence**

dim_customers has 1319 rows while stg_customers has 1500 rows. The missing 181 customers cause 1892 orders to have null region_name. The loss is due to inner joins dropping customers without matching nation or region. Changing joins to left joins will preserve customers and propagate nulls, fixing the issue without data loss.
