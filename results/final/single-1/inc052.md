## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.orders`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`, `stg_orders`

**Evidence**

Row count matches between raw.orders and staging.stg_orders (14856). All order dates are between 1992-01-01 and 1998-07-12, with zero rows where o_orderdate > CURRENT_DATE. The failing check expects recent orders (order_date > CURRENT_DATE) but source data contains no such dates, indicating the source is not delivering current data rather than a model bug.
