**Status:** Escalated to the owner of raw.orders. Nothing was changed.

## What broke

- `assert_orders_fresh` fail (1 rows): Got 1 result, configured to fail if != 0

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.orders`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`, `stg_orders`

**Evidence**

The freshness check (assert_orders_fresh) is failing because the maximum order_date in both raw.orders and staging.stg_orders is 1998-06-18, which is far older than the expected recent date range (e.g., within the last 30 days). Row counts match between raw and staging, and no nulls or key issues are observed, indicating the source data itself is stale.
