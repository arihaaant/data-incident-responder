## What broke

- `not_null_fct_orders_region_name` fail (14744 rows): Got 14744 results, configured to fail if != 0

## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `model.dim_customers`
- **Cause:** a change in a model
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`

**Evidence**

The not_null_fct_orders_region_name test failed with 14744 nulls. fct_orders left joins dim_customers for region_name. dim_customers had many missing rows because its joins were wrong: it joined stg_customers to stg_nations on customer_key = nation_key and used an inner join to stg_regions, dropping most customers. Correcting to left joins on proper foreign keys restores region_name for all orders, fixing the test.
