## Diagnosis

- **Started at:** `source.raw.orders`
- **Cause:** data arriving from upstream
- **Downstream models affected:** `fct_daily_revenue`, `fct_orders`, `stg_orders`

**Evidence**

Staging model stg_orders just selects _loaded_at unchanged, so the staleness originates in raw.orders. The upstream ETL that populates raw.orders is not delivering new data.
